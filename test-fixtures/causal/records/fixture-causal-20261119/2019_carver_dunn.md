# Diabetes mellitus, physical activity and the fixture outcome: a matched case-control study

## Abstract

We compared 240 adults who developed the fixture outcome (FO) within 30 days
of elective fixture surgery with 480 matched controls from two fixture
centres. Diabetes mellitus was less common among cases than controls, and
leisure-time physical activity was associated with lower odds of FO.

## Methods

Cases were adults with FO within 30 days of elective fixture surgery; each was
matched to two controls on age, sex and centre. Leisure-time physical activity
was recorded in hours per week. Conditional logistic regression was adjusted
for age, sex and body mass index.

## Results

Diabetes mellitus was less common among cases than controls (adjusted OR 0.7,
95% CI 0.5–0.98). Each additional hour per week of leisure-time physical
activity was associated with lower odds of FO (adjusted OR 0.92, 95% CI
0.86–0.98). Patients with comorbidities had higher odds of FO (adjusted OR
1.8, 95% CI 1.2–2.7).

## Discussion

Unlike earlier cohorts, we found diabetes mellitus to be protective, which may
reflect closer perioperative glucose monitoring in diabetic patients than in
non-diabetic patients.
