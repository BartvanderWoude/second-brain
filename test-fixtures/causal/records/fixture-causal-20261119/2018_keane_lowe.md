## Abstract

In a cross-sectional survey of 500 adults after elective fixture surgery at
one fixture centre, 200 (40%) had diabetes. Diabetes was strongly associated
with the fixture outcome (OR 3.5, 95% CI 2.0–6.1). Higher body mass index was
associated with diabetes (OR 1.9 per 5 kg/m², 95% CI 1.5–2.4).
