# Causal pathways to the fixture outcome after outpatient fixture procedures: a registry cohort

## Abstract

We used a national registry of 8,400 adults undergoing outpatient fixture
procedures to estimate the effects of DM, BMI, physical activity and operative
duration on the fixture outcome within 90 days.

## Methods

Figure 1 shows the directed acyclic graph we drew to select adjustment
variables for the fixture outcome. BMI was measured in kg/m², physical
activity in MET-hours per week and operative duration in minutes. Every model
was adjusted for the minimal adjustment set the graph implies.

## Results

Of the 8,400 patients, 2,100 (25%) had DM. DM was associated with the fixture
outcome (adjusted OR 1.6, 95% CI 1.2–2.1). Physical activity was not
associated with the fixture outcome (adjusted OR 0.95, 95% CI 0.70–1.30). In
the first postoperative year, a higher BMI predicted lower subsequent physical
activity (standardized beta −0.21, 95% CI −0.27 to −0.15). Operative duration
was a significant predictor of the fixture outcome (p = 0.01).
