## Abstract

Background: The fixture outcome is a common complication of elective fixture
surgery. Methods: In this double-blind trial, 3,000 adults undergoing elective
fixture surgery at four fixture centres were randomized to fixture
prophylaxis or placebo and followed for 30 days. Results: The postoperative
fixture outcome occurred in 120 of 1,500 patients (8%) in the placebo group
and in 75 of 1,500 patients (5%) in the prophylaxis group. Fixture prophylaxis
reduced the risk of the postoperative fixture outcome (RR 0.63, 95% CI
0.47–0.83). Conclusion: Fixture prophylaxis prevents the postoperative
fixture outcome.
