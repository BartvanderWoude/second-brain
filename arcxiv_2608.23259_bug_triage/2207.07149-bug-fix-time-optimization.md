## Problem addressed

Manual triage is slow and can overload individual developers. The paper frames assignment as resource allocation with both time and workload objectives.

## Method

Matrix factorization produces developer recommendations, Gale-Shapley performs matching, and differential evolution tunes developer-profile weights.

## Result

Reported fixing-time reductions versus manual triage were 80.67% for Linux, 23.61% for Eclipse, and 60.22% for Apache, with uniform workload.

## Synthesis

**Why relevant** — It supplies an optimization-based alternative to classifier-only assignment.

**What would need to change to apply it** — A new setting needs trustworthy historical fixing-time data and explicit rules for workload and developer availability.

**Skeptical note** — Comparisons against manual triage may depend strongly on reconstruction assumptions and historical data quality.
