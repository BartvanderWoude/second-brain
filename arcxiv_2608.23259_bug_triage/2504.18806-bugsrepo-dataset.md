## Problem addressed

Bug-analysis systems need datasets that combine report text with status, discussion, contributor, and quality information.

## Method

BugsRepo aggregates Mozilla Bugzilla metadata, comments, contributor activity, and a CTQRS-filtered structured-report subset.

## Result

The dataset contains 119,585 fixed or closed reports, 19,351 contributor profiles, and 10,351 structured reports.

## Synthesis

**Why relevant** — It offers inputs for training and evaluating several stages of automated triage within one ecosystem.

**What would need to change to apply it** — Models trained on Mozilla data need external validation and a mapping from Bugzilla fields to the target tracker.

**Skeptical note** — One community's workflows, contributor roles, and labeling practices may create strong project-specific bias.
