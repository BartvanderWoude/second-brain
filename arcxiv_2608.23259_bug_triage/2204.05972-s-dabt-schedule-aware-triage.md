## Problem addressed

Bug assignment often ignores developer availability and blocking dependencies, producing slow or infeasible schedules.

## Method

S-DABT combines learned assignment signals with an integer-programming formulation that includes developer schedules, fixing costs, and bug dependencies.

## Result

Simulations on EclipseJDT, LibreOffice, GCC, and Mozilla report lower fixing time, better assignment accuracy, fairer utilization, and fewer dependency-related infeasible assignments.

## Synthesis

**Why relevant** — It treats triage as constrained scheduling rather than text classification alone.

**What would need to change to apply it** — Deployment requires reliable schedule, cost, and dependency data that may not exist in every tracker.

**Skeptical note** — Simulation gains may not transfer directly to live teams whose availability and priorities change continuously.
