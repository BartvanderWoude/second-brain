## Problem addressed

The TianoCore community has many untriaged firmware issues and a slow manual process spanning validity, duplication, priority, and assignment decisions.

## Method

TianoForge applies GPT models, with and without RAG, across the four triage subtasks in one integrated workflow.

## Result

The experimental study reports a reduction in average triage time from roughly 11 days to approximately 7 minutes, or 99.95%.

## Synthesis

**Why relevant** — It is the anchor paper for testing an end-to-end automated triage profile and links several narrower methods in this collection.

**What would need to change to apply it** — Transfer requires a target project's taxonomy, historical decisions, assignee structure, retrieval corpus, and task-specific quality gates.

**Skeptical note** — A dramatic time reduction does not by itself establish that automated validity, duplication, priority, and assignment decisions match expert quality.
