## Problem addressed

Kubernetes incident diagnosis requires correlating signals across tools, slowing triage and increasing alert fatigue.

## Method

ARGUS connects an LLM to Kubernetes, Prometheus, Loki, and NATS through MCP servers and posts structured diagnostics into Slack.

## Result

Across ten controlled incidents, ARGUS named every root cause correctly and achieved a 0.91 aggregate MCP success ratio; six interviewed engineers trusted diagnostics more than recommended fixes.

## Synthesis

**Why relevant** — It demonstrates standardized tool grounding for operational triage and separates diagnostic confidence from action recommendations.

**What would need to change to apply it** — A deployment needs organization-specific observability permissions, tool schemas, audit logs, and safe handling of live incident data.

**Skeptical note** — Ten controlled scenarios and six interviews are preliminary evidence, especially for the safety of prescriptive recommendations.
